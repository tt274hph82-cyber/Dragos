## Channel description
What if your pet swallowed your mom? What if your dad became the final boss? What if you never stopped playing?

Blocky What If turns one crazy question into a fast, funny animated story, with blocky characters, big twists and an ending you won't see coming. 🎮

🆕 New stories every week: Shorts and long videos.
💬 Comment your own "What if…" and it might be the next episode!
🔔 Subscribe so you don't miss one.
