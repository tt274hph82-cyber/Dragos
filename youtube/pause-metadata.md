🔬 Is any of this real?
• Most people can hold their breath for about 30 to 90 seconds.
• The world record is 29 minutes 3 seconds, set by Vitomir Maričić in 2025 after breathing pure oxygen (Guinness World Records).
• The urge to breathe comes mostly from carbon dioxide building up in your blood, not from running out of oxygen.
• Breathing fast and deep before holding your breath delays that alarm, and it can cause sudden blackout underwater, called hypoxic blackout (American Red Cross, YMCA of the USA and USA Swimming joint statement; StatPearls).
• 100 one-minute pauses a day = 36,500 minutes a year ≈ 25 extra days.

⚠️ Never practise holding your breath underwater or alone. Hypoxic blackout has no warning signs.
