## Description (copy everything between the lines)
---
In 1986, Pripyat was one of the youngest cities in the Soviet Union. Its average resident was about 26. By the evening of 27 April, almost 50,000 people had been told to pack for three days. None of them came back to live there.

Pripyat wasn't just a ghost town. It was a company town, built for a single employer: the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant. And it was one of a whole family of Soviet "atomgrads", atom-cities built to serve nuclear plants. This documentary tells the story of Pripyat and of the sister cities it left behind: Slavutych, Visaginas, Energodar and Sosnovy Bor.
