✅ Key takeaways
• Shorts are for discovery: fast reach, quick tests, new viewers
• Long videos are for depth: trust, search traffic, loyal fans and steadier income
• A Short view can be a second of attention. Views are not the same as fans
• YPP: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (12 months) or 10M Shorts views (90 days)
• Cut Shorts from your long videos and use the related video link
• Keep topics matched: a cooking Short should lead to a cooking video
• Simple plan: 1 long video + 3 Shorts from it, filmed on one day
• Don't judge a Short in its first hour

Maya and Leo are made-up examples, and the numbers shown on screen are illustrative.
