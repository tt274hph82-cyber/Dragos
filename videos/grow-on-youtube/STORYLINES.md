# How to Grow on YouTube — storylines (~8 min, 16:9, narrated)

## A. Channel Zero (recommended)
Device: one creator's channel worked through, start to finish. Form: a flight through one world.
We follow "Maya", a made-up beginner with 0 subscribers, across her first year. Every lesson is a problem she hits on her own desk and screen, then fixes; her (clearly illustrative) dashboard numbers move as she learns.
First 2 s: a phone screen shows a fresh upload with "3 views", the counter stuck.
Beats: stuck at 3 views → why YouTube recommends videos → pick a niche → titles + thumbnails (the click) → the first 30 seconds (the hook) → keeping people watching → consistency + Shorts → reading analytics → building a community → one year later → the one idea.
Look: warm desk-at-night world, a phone and a laptop as the "screens", a red-orange accent from your existing reel, big clean sans type.
Sound: Kokoro voice narration (free, on-device), soft lo-fi bed, small UI clicks/pops on actions.

## B. The Click Machine
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one continuous take along a machine.
A big machine on the screen turns "impressions" into "views" into "fans". Each station (show → click → watch → stay → come back) is a lesson, and the camera rides along it while dots flow through and drop out at weak stations.
First 2 s: thousands of dots pour into the machine; almost all fall out at the first gate.
Beats: the funnel → impressions → click (titles/thumbnails) → hook → retention → session/next video → subscribe + return → tuning the machine with analytics → end.
Look: clean bright diagram world, one accent colour, labels on the parts they name.
Sound: narration, ticking mechanical underscore, dot "pops".

## C. Every Second Counts (bold)
Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
Eight chapters of giant kinetic type, built from the same dark + orange style and countdown clock as your 10-second reel, so it reads as its big sibling.
First 2 s: "YOU HAVE 8 MINUTES" slams in; the clock starts counting down.
Beats: chapter cards 1–8, each a short punchy rule with one visual proof, clock running the whole time → "Used well."
Look: your existing reel's palette (#0B0B0F, #FF5A1F, #F5F3EE), Inter Display.
Sound: narration over a driving beat, hard cuts on the beat.

Assumptions: 7.5–8.5 min, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, free Kokoro narration and free local music (HeyGen voices/music are the paid option).
Numbers shown on screen are illustrative, not real stats.
