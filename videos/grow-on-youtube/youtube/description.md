✅ Quick checklist
• Pick one clear viewer and make videos for them
• Thumbnail: one subject, strong contrast, words you can read on a phone
• Title: spark curiosity, never lie
• Open with the payoff, cut the long intro
• Find the biggest dip in your retention graph and fix that moment next time
• Upload on a schedule you can keep
• Use Shorts to find new viewers, long videos to keep them
• Every week, check click-through rate, average view duration and returning viewers
• Reply to comments, and turn the best questions into videos

Maya is a made-up example, and the numbers shown on screen are illustrative.
