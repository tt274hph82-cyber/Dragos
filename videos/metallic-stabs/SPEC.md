# SPEC — Metallic Stabs (music video)

GOAL: A 3:35 dark-tech music video where every section of the song is its own machine world, escalating to the final hit. No section reuses another's picture; the only callback is the 7-second overload montage before the end.
OUTPUT: HyperFrames, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, one generative <canvas> driven by time (hf-seek), the original track as audio.
TIME: 215.2 s. Tempo measured at ~128.15 BPM, first beat 0.419 s (beat 0.468 s, bar 1.873 s).

LOOK: ground #050608. Steel: gradients #2b2f36 → #cfd6df → #4a515b. Accents: cyan #19e6ff (signal), red #ff2d3d (stabs), amber #ffb020 (sparks). Type: JetBrains Mono (terminal), Archivo Black (title). Light grain, vignette. No logos, no lyrics.

SECTIONS (from `hyperframes audio`, snapped to beats):
- 0.0–7.4 BOOT: terminal lines type in, a scanline sweeps, title assembles from particles.
- 7.4–22.9 CIRCUIT: traces grow out of a chip; pulses ride the traces on beats; slow push-in.
- 22.9–30.5 CHARGE: chrome rings spin up faster and faster into a white-out.
- 30.5–60.5 PRESS (drop 1): steel blades slam in on every beat with sparks; the pattern changes every 4 bars; dip at 56–59 = blades retract, sparks rain.
- 60.5–105.4 TUNNEL: wireframe tunnel flies at the camera; shape changes every 8 bars; dip at 86–89 = stop, single ring.
- 105.4–120.4 RACKS: corridor of server racks; LED patterns on the beat; red laser scan per bar.
- 120.4–150.2 CORE (breakdown): rotating wireframe sphere, dust; shockwaves on the hits at 135 and 142; build from 144.
- 150.2–175.8 SHATTER (drop 2): the sphere explodes into a 12-fold kaleidoscope of blades; dip 163–164 freezes it.
- 175.8–194.9 CITY: signal glitch, then a grid city of towers rising on the beat as the camera flies over.
- 194.9–201.9 OVERLOAD: one beat each of earlier worlds, remixed and colour-split.
- 201.9–209.3 POWER DOWN: lines retract; the scanline and terminal return (ending rhymes with the opening).
- 209.3–215.2 FINAL HIT: the title is stamped in steel with a spark burst, then black.

SAFETY: full-frame flashes only at 30.5, 150.2, 194.9 and 210.0, each decaying over 0.3 s (well under 3 flashes per second).
BANNED: spectrum bars or waveforms as the main picture; the same treatment for more than ~6 s without change; logos; lyrics.
