✅ Thumbnail checklist
• Shrink it to phone size: can you get it in one glance?
• One clear subject, big in the frame
• Strong contrast: light on dark, warm on cool (check it in black and white)
• 3–4 big words at most, and don't repeat the title
• If you show your face, make the emotion big and honest
• Tell a tiny story: before and after, or a question
• Study the top thumbnails in your niche, then do one thing differently
• Build a consistent look: same colours, same font, same layout
• Only promise what the video delivers
• Test versions with Test & Compare, and refresh a thumbnail that's struggling
• Size: 1280 × 720 px, 16:9, under 2 MB
