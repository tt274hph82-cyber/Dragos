Facts and numbers I assumed

  • Date and place: November–December 1932, the wheat belt of Western Australia (Campion district).
  • "About 20,000 emus" is a contemporary estimate, not a count.
  • Major G. P. W. Meredith led the operation with two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds of ammunition.
  • 9,860 rounds for 986 kills is Meredith's own report; historians treat these exact tens as suspiciously neat, so the play says "reported".
  • Emus can run at about 50 km/h. One gun jammed during an ambush, and a gun mounted on a truck could not keep up over rough ground.
  • The army withdrew in December 1932; "Emus 1, Army 0" is the play's joke, not an official score.
