The Pig War

San Juan Island, 1859: Britain and America both claim it, a pig eats a farmer's potatoes, he shoots the pig, and two empires end up in a standoff that lasts twelve years. Facts and numbers I assumed

  • 15 June 1859: American farmer Lyman Cutlar shot a Hudson's Bay Company pig that was rooting in his potato patch.
  • British officials threatened to arrest him; Captain George Pickett landed with 64 U.S. soldiers (27 July 1859).
  • Britain sent three warships at first; the standoff grew to several hundred U.S. troops facing over 2,000 British sailors and marines. I avoid more exact counts (some sources say 461 and 14 cannon), because they vary.
  • Rear Admiral Robert Baynes refused to start a war "over a squabble about a pig". "Not over a pig." is the play's shortening, shown as his quote.
  • Both sides then held the island together for 12 years; in 1872 Kaiser Wilhelm I, as arbiter, awarded it to the United States.
  • The only casualty of the "war" was the pig.
