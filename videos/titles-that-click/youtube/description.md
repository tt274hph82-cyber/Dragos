✅ Key takeaways
• A title is a promise: make it clear, interesting, and keep it
• Put the best words first; aim for under 60 characters
• Be specific: numbers, times, prices, results
• Make it about the viewer, not about you
• Open a loop, and answer it in the video
• Use feeling words like salt, and skip all caps titles
• For search, use the words people type (check the search bar suggestions)
• Don't repeat your thumbnail text: the thumbnail shows, the title tells
• Write 10 titles, pick 1, and watch your click-through rate in YouTube Studio

5 formulas
1. How to [result] without [pain]
2. I tried [thing] for [time]
3. [Number] mistakes
4. Why [surprising thing]
5. [This] vs. [that]
